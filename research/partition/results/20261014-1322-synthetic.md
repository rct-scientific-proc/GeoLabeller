## Clustering: one verdict per cluster

Purity is the accuracy if each cluster took its majority verdict; clicks is how many verdicts that is (an unclustered HDBSCAN chip is a click of its own). The true number of kinds is what the verdicts say; the estimated one is chosen by silhouette, as it would have to be in use.
